About Us

Welcome to Cowboys Corner Cafe

We are a friendly, local, family owned and operated breakfast and lunch spot in Watsonville, serving hearty comfort food and fresh-made favorites every day. We’re known for generous portions, welcoming service, and a relaxed western-style atmosphere. Whether you’re stopping in for coffee or a full meal, you’ll always feel right at home at our table.
